Official Technical Documentation

ReadSmart System Feature Manual & Snippets

A comprehensive catalog of functional screen captures and descriptions covering every feature per user type (Student, Teacher, and System Administrator) for Pedro Victorina Calo Elementary School.

Application Name ReadSmart Platform
Target Users Pupils & Faculty (Grade 3)
Curriculum Base DepEd Reading Standards
Core Technologies Next.js, Supabase, TTS Web Speech

🔐 Part 1: Authentication & Access Control

01. Unified Secure Sign-In Portal

/login
All User Roles

Serves as the gateway to ReadSmart, authenticating students and faculty members with credential validation and role-based redirecting.

  • Encrypted session management via Supabase Auth
  • Automatic routing: students to /dashboard and teachers to /teacher
  • Helpful validation prompts for invalid or unverified accounts

02. Student Self-Enrollment & Section Assignment

/signup
Pupil Registration

Enables grade school pupils to create their learner accounts and automatically link to their official class section and teacher advisory.

  • Selectable class sections (Grade 3-A, Grade 3-B, Grade 3-C, etc.)
  • Initializes student reading profile with 0 XP, default streak, and mascot character
  • Immediate feedback with pre-filled login credentials post-creation

🎒 Part 2: Student (Pupil) Portal Features

03. Student Home Learning Dashboard

/dashboard
Student Portal

The main student command center providing quick access to active reading lessons, daily reading streaks, and accumulated experience points (XP).

  • Live daily reading streak counter (🔥) to encourage consistent practice
  • "Continue Reading" card pointing directly to the current in-progress story
  • Quick glance at earned badges and classroom XP standings

04. 5-Stage Storybook Mastery Pathway

/dashboard (Pathway Overview)
Student Portal

Visualizes sequential reading progression across 5 structured achievement stages: 1st Star, 2nd Ribbon, 3rd Bronze, 4th Silver, and 5th Gold.

  • Progress percentage tracking toward active badge goals
  • Clear visual feedback on mastered vs. locked stages
  • DepEd-aligned reading difficulty ramp-up

05. Storybook Library & Chapter Catalogue

/dashboard/lessons
Student Portal

Categorized collection of all curated story lessons, allowing learners to browse passages by difficulty level (Easy, Medium, Hard).

  • Illustrated story cards with vocabulary count and reading time estimates
  • Status indicators showing Completed, Ready to Read, or Locked
  • Search and filter options by chapter theme or grade level

06. Chapter Story Checklist & Checkpoints

/dashboard/lessons (Module Breakdown)
Student Portal

Breaks each chapter into bite-sized stories that must be read before unlocking the Stage Final Assessment and badge accolades.

  • Score history badge per story (e.g. 100% Score)
  • "Re-read" and "Retake" buttons for self-paced reinforcement
  • Locked milestone safeguard ensuring thorough comprehension before advancing

07. Living Storybook Interactive Reader

/dashboard/lessons/[id]
Core Function

The flagship reading experience featuring custom scene illustrations, synchronized sentence progression, and assistive AI narration.

  • Read Aloud (TTS): Browser speech synthesis pronouncing sentences naturally
  • Click-to-Pronounce: Underlined vocabulary words can be tapped for instant phonics audio
  • Sentence-by-sentence pacing with visual progress bar (e.g., Sentence 2/18)

08. In-Story Vocabulary Callout & Phonics Support

/dashboard/lessons/[id] (Interactive Modal)
Core Function

Provides immediate vocabulary scaffolding whenever a pupil encounters an unfamiliar word during active reading.

  • Kid-friendly English and contextual definitions
  • Audio playback button for slow and clear word pronunciation
  • Contextual sentence usage helping children understand word meaning in story context

09. Interactive Comprehension Assessment

/dashboard/quiz/[id]
Assessment

Evaluates reading recall, context clues, and moral understanding through multiple-choice questions configured by teachers.

  • Distraction-free question interface with single-choice selection
  • "Need a reading hint?" assistance link to guide struggling pupils
  • Clear XP bounty indicators (+10 XP to +20 XP per item)

10. Instant Evaluation, XP Reward & Educational Rationale

/dashboard/quiz/[id] (Answer Verification)
Assessment

Provides immediate positive reinforcement upon answer submission, explaining why an answer is correct to solidify comprehension.

  • Instant XP point credit with celebratory animations
  • Teacher-authored explanation explaining the key theme or fact from the passage
  • Real-time score logging to teacher database for comprehension analytics

11. Trophy Room & Milestone Badges Showcase

/dashboard/badges
Gamification

Showcases unlocked and upcoming accolades earned by completing stories and demonstrating high comprehension accuracy.

  • Interactive badge map with visual medals (Star, Ribbon, Bronze, Silver, Gold)
  • Detailed criteria inspection for locked badges
  • Motivates children through gamified educational recognition

12. Classroom Champions Podium & Leaderboard

/dashboard/leaderboard
Gamification

Displays ranking standings within the student's enrolled section based on total XP and consistent reading streaks.

  • Elevated Top 3 podium (1st Gold Crown, 2nd Silver, 3rd Bronze)
  • Personalized "Your Current Standing" banner highlighting remaining XP to advance
  • Scope toggle between Section-wide and School-wide ranks

13. Official Star Reader Certificate of Completion

/dashboard/quiz (Grand Milestone Modal)
Accreditation

An official DepEd Region XIII compliant certificate generated upon mastering all 5 Story Realm stages.

  • Personalized with pupil's name, school name, section, and date of award
  • "Print Award" function generating a physical diploma ready for framing
  • Audio applause celebration to reward academic perseverance

14. Settings Hub & 24 Illustrated Kid Avatar Selector

/dashboard/settings?tab=account
Personalization

Allows young learners to personalize their reading identity by selecting from 24 custom-illustrated, diverse boy and girl character portraits.

  • Real-time avatar synchronization across header, leaderboard, and certificates
  • AI Voice narration speed and pitch adjustment controls in voice tab
  • Friendly profile overview with class section and learner ID

👩‍🏫 Part 3: Teacher (Faculty) Portal Features

15. Grade 3 Classroom Reading Hub & Classroom Analytics

/teacher
Teacher Portal

Executive dashboard providing real-time data on class-wide comprehension benchmarks, active accounts, and stage mastery distribution.

  • Class Comprehension Rate gauge (compared to the DepEd ≥70% passing threshold)
  • Section switcher filtering analytics across Grade 3-A, 3-B, 3-C, and Twilight
  • Live pupil progression distribution tracking students along the 5-stage pathway

16. Pupil Early-Intervention & Attention Radar

/teacher (Intervention Section)
Teacher Portal

An automated alert system that detects struggling readers needing phonics assistance, score recovery, or reading intervention.

  • Automated triage badges: Critical, Watchlist, and Mastering & On Track
  • Actionable recommendations (e.g. "Challenge with Next Stage Stories" or "Provide Remedial Phonics")
  • Quick "Send Praise" or "Send Guidance" note trigger

17. Pupil Enrollment & Section Roster Management

/teacher/students
Administration

Comprehensive roster table displaying individual learner metrics including comprehension scores, reading speed (WPM), and quiz counts.

  • Individual student status monitoring with section tags and pupil IDs
  • "Export Roster (CSV)" function for school administrative reports
  • "+ Enroll New Student" action for quick classroom registration

18. Reading Passages & Story Curriculum Manager

/teacher/lessons
Curriculum Authoring

Empowers teachers to manage and customize the story reading curriculum stored in Supabase.

  • Publish / Unpublish toggle to pace lesson releases per class section
  • Audio narration and reading threshold configuration (e.g., Pass ≥ 70%)
  • Assign stories to specific class sections or core curriculum

19. Comprehension Question Bank & Assessment Hub

/teacher/quizzes
Assessment Control

A centralized repository for reviewing, editing, and authoring comprehension quiz questions for every story passage.

  • Story-by-story question filter to inspect questions assigned to any story
  • Correct answer key validation badges and point weight adjustments
  • Customization of teacher explanations and student hints

20. Question Authoring & Item Calibration Modal

/teacher/quizzes (Authoring Dialog)
Assessment Control

A guided form allowing faculty to craft high-standard DepEd comprehension questions with custom choices, hints, and explanations.

  • Radio button selection to specify the single verified correct answer key
  • Mandatory teacher explanation field supporting educational rationale
  • Optional student hint field to assist struggling readers without giving away answers
