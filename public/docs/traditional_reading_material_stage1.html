PART II: TRADITIONAL READING PASSAGE (Stage 1 Stories) Read Aloud / Silent
Direksyon sa Pagbasa: Basaha ang tulo (3) ka mugbong istorya sa ubos uban sa saktong paglitok sa mga pulong.

Story 1: Pat and the Cat

Pat has a fat cat. The cat sat on a soft mat.

A big rat ran by the mat. The cat saw the rat run. Words like rat, cat, and mat rhyme with the same sound!

The cat ran fast to catch the rat. Pat fed his cat in a red pan.

Story 2: The Beautiful Garden

One sunny morning, Mia and her friend Leo decided to visit the garden near the park. Before they left, Mia's mom asked them to find beautiful flowers.

When they arrived, fresh flowers were blooming everywhere! Leo asked if they could find a rose first, and Mia smiled happily.

They walked around the big fountain looking for roses. Finally, they found a rose bush, took pictures, and laughed together.

Story 3: Pat and Mia in the Park

Pat took his cat to the green park. Mia and Leo were there smelling the pink roses.

The fat cat ran under a tall wooden bench. Mia gave the cat a soft pat on the back.

They were glad to play in the warm sun. Pat and Mia walked home with big smiles.
